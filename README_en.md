# History of Vietnam

This repository is used to store historical events of Vietnam.

Events are aggregated from various sources across the Internet.

## 📜 Event Selection Criteria

This repository prioritizes aggregating and selecting events based on the following standardized criteria:

### 1. Inclusion Criteria
* **Major Historical Milestones**: Political, military, diplomatic, economic, social, or cultural events that have a significant transformational impact on Vietnam's history, national sovereignty, or international standing.
* **Historic Firsts**: Important inaugural milestones of the country (e.g., Declaration of Independence, first Constitution, first General Election, establishment of the National Assembly, major first victories).
* **Milestones of Prominent Figures**: Events closely associated with the life and career of national heroes, leaders, and historical figures (such as revolutionary activity milestones, campaign leadership, major achievements, heroic sacrifices, or dates of passing).

### 2. Exclusion Rules
* **Minor Personal Biographical Details**: Do not include private life information, birthplace, childhood details, or personal information with little historical value.
* **Unnecessary Detailed Addresses / Street Names**: Avoid recording specific street numbers or street names unless the location itself is a famous historical landmark (e.g., Ba Dinh Square, Nha Rong Wharf).
* **Minor Commemorative or Honoring Activities**: Limit procedural events such as naming schools/streets, inaugurating local memorial plaques, or small-scale anniversaries.
* **Routine Administrative Events**: Exclude internal meetings, periodic administrative announcements, or international events without direct impact on Vietnam.

### 3. Editorial Principles
* **Objective and Neutral**: Record events based on historical facts, respecting the principle of neutrality without revisionism.
* **Third-Person Perspective**: Use precise terminology (e.g., "Vietnam", "the Vietnamese people" instead of "our country", "our troops").
* **Concise and Contextual**: Each entry clearly explains the progression and significance of the event without being verbose.
* **Authentic Timeline & Deduplication**: Only store events with clear timestamps; duplicate or overlapping events are merged into a single entry.
